[abstract]
--
TaskGraph is the workflow capability of GroovyConcurrentUtils: a Groovy DSL for describing a
graph of tasks, and the engine that runs it with retries, gateways, timeouts, cancellation,
persistence and events.

This is the TaskGraph part of _GroovyConcurrentUtils: Capabilities and Reference_, published
on its own. The pools, promises and dataflow variables that TaskGraph is built on, and the
library's configuration, security and clustering services, are described in the full book.
--
